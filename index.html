One Waits Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how One Waits (Android package com.vsgamerz.onewaits), a puzzle game by VS Gamerz, handles your information. VS Gamerz is an individual developer, not a company. In this policy, "we" means VS Gamerz.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.
  • The game has no ads. It does not use your device's advertising ID.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It contains:

  • which levels you have cleared, your stars, best number of taps and number of clears;
  • your hint count and the day you last got a free hint;
  • your settings (music, sound, vibration, reduce motion);
  • the level you are currently playing, if you leave it unfinished;
  • a few internal counters, such as the day you first played.

This file stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in Android cloud backups.

Information collected by Google Firebase

The game includes two Firebase services from Google. Google processes this information for us, to provide these services. It is not used for advertising.

Firebase Analytics

Used to see which levels are played, cleared or left, how hints are used, and how many people keep playing. It collects:

  • game events that we send: tutorial completed; level started, completed or left (level number, attempt number, whether it is a replay, stars, number of taps, time taken, hints used, and the reason for leaving, such as restart or exit); and hint used (level number and hints left);
  • events Firebase records automatically, such as first open, session start and time in the app;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and country or region (estimated from your IP address; Analytics does not store the IP address itself);
  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase for this installation of the game.

Analytics in this game does not collect your device's advertising ID or Android ID. We have turned that collection off.

Firebase Crashlytics

Used to find and fix crashes. If the game crashes, it sends a crash report with the technical details of the crash (stack trace), the state of the app at the time, device model, operating system version, app version, the level number being played, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash data is kept for up to 90 days.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use information

Only to run, fix and improve the game: to find crashes, and to see which levels are too hard or too easy. We don't use it to identify you, and we don't use it for advertising.

Sharing

We don't sell your data and we don't share it with third parties. Google processes Analytics and Crashlytics data on our behalf, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to send analytics and crash reports.
  • Vibrate: for short vibrations while you play. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

The Firebase libraries also add a few system permissions they need to work: keeping the device awake briefly while data is sent (wake lock), reading which app installed the game (Play Install Referrer), and Android's privacy-preserving ad-measurement permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION). These are not used to show ads. The game has no ads, and the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is removed from the game.

Links to other services

The Rate us and Privacy options in Settings open the Google Play Store app or your web browser. Those apps are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. One Waits does not send them any information about you.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Uninstalling One Waits deletes its save file from your device. You can also clear it at any time in your device's Settings > Apps > One Waits > Storage > Clear storage.
  • To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your installation, email us at the address below. We'll ask Google to delete it within 30 days. Because we don't know who you are, tell us roughly when you played and on what device so we can find it.
  • The game does not currently have an in-app switch to turn off analytics. If you don't want analytics or crash data sent, you can uninstall the game.

Children

One Waits is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if advertising is added), we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
