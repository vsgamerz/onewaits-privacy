One Waits – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how One Waits (Android package com.vsgamerz.onewaits), a puzzle game by VS Gamerz, handles your information.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. Analytics events are only sent after the consent step is finished and allows it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it's required, we ask for your consent first using Google's consent tool. Where consent was asked, you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It contains your cleared levels, stars and best taps, your hint count, your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion) and any unfinished level. This file is not sent to us.

Information collected by third-party services

The game includes software from Google. These services collect some information automatically when you play.

Firebase Analytics (Google)

Used to see which levels are played, cleared or skipped, how hints and ads are used, and how many people keep playing. It collects:

  • game events, such as level started or completed, stars, hints used and ad views;
  • device and app information, such as device model, OS version, app version, language and country/region (from your IP address, which is not stored by Analytics);
  • an app-instance identifier and, where allowed, your device's advertising ID. The game turns off Analytics collection of the Android ID (SSAID).

Until the consent step is finished, Analytics events wait on your device. If the consent step does not allow data collection, those events are discarded and not sent.

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

Used to find and fix crashes. When the game crashes or has a serious error, it sends a crash report with the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, the level being played, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Google AdMob and consent (Google)

The game shows full-screen ads between some levels (not during your first session or before levels 1 to 5 are cleared, and at most once every 3 level ends and 2 minutes apart), plus optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a hint. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location (city/region level);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or watched) and device and app information.

Consent: where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested, and ads are only requested once that consent step is finished. If you don't consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads. If the consent step can't be completed, the game doesn't request ads. Where consent was asked, you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game (this option only appears in regions where the consent message applies).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use information

We use this information only to run, fix and improve the game, to understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and to show ads that keep the game free. We don't use it to identify you personally.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. Information is processed by Google (Firebase and AdMob) as described above. Advertising data is used by Google and its ad partners to serve and measure ads, in line with your consent choices. We may disclose information if required by law.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed for ads, analytics and crash reporting.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used for ads and analytics, as described above.
  • Vibrate: for short vibrations while you play. You can turn it off in Settings.

The Google Firebase and AdMob libraries also add system permissions they need to work: keeping the device awake briefly while data is sent (wake lock), running short background work (foreground service), reading which app installed the game (Play Install Referrer), Android's privacy-preserving ad permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), and an internal permission that only this app can use.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Where consent was asked, change it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes its save data from your device.
  • To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, email us at the address below. Because we don't know who you are, tell us roughly when you played and on what device so we can find it. We'll ask Google to delete it within 30 days.

Children

One Waits is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
