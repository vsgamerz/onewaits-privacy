One Waits Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game One Waits (Android package com.vsgamerz.onewaits) handles information. One Waits is a free puzzle game made by VS Gamerz, an individual developer. It is not made by a company. In this policy, "we" and "the developer" mean VS Gamerz.

Summary

  • One Waits does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the game.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes its data.

Information we collect

None. One Waits does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and it does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The game does not use the internet to send or receive data.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It contains:

  • which levels you have cleared, your stars and best number of taps;
  • your hint count;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion);
  • the level you are currently playing, if you leave it unfinished.

This file stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in Android cloud backups.

Permissions

One Waits asks for one Android permission: vibrate, used for short vibrations when you play. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

Links to other services

The Rate us and Privacy options in Settings open the Google Play Store app or your web browser. Those apps are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. One Waits does not send them any information about you.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling One Waits deletes all of its data from your device. You can also clear it at any time in your device's Settings > Apps > One Waits > Storage > Clear storage.

Children

One Waits is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if advertising is added), we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
